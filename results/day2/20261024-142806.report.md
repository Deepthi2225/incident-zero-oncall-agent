Calling payment 'resolved' therefore rests on thin evidence. (E17, E18)

- Objection: E18 is cited as support, but the checkout->payment client/server latency gap (about 105 ms) matches the checkout->shipping gap (about 83 ms, E15), and shipping isn't implicated at all. The gap looks like normal overhead, not a sign tied to payment. (E18, E15)

That fits payment being missing from the Docker network or a DNS fault better than the container simply being stopped. Separately, the frontend-proxy egress failures (E16) are still unexplained.
